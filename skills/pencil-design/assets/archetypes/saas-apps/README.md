# saas-apps

Archetypes for SaaS product surfaces, split by buyer audience.

- **b2b/**, products sold to other businesses for internal use. Trustworthy, dense, often workflow-led.
- **b2b2b/**, developer-facing platforms and infrastructure tooling. Technical credibility leads; the buyer is an engineer.
- **b2c/**, consumer-facing products. Soft surfaces, conversational copy, image-led where content is the point.

The user's brief usually signals which subfolder applies: words like *enterprise*, *workspace*, *team plan* point at b2b; *developer*, *API*, *infrastructure* point at b2b2b; *personal*, *individual*, *daily use* point at b2c.

If you can't tell, ask.
